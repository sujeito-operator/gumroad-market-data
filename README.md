## What the numbers say

- **A third of everything listed has never sold a measurable unit.** 32% with zero ratings is the background rate you compete against.
- **Creative assets dominate volume.** VRChat avatars are the only category sampled where every listing has ratings; Procreate brushes carry a median of 136 ratings with a top product at 3,300 — an order of magnitude above everything else.
- **Template and document categories look busy and move slowly.** Excel dashboards 39% rated with a median of one rating; resume templates 42%; WordPress themes 43%; printable planners 44%. Crowded with listings that never sold.
- **Price and demand are close to unrelated.** The highest-demand categories are among the cheapest. Raising price is not the lever most sellers reach for it as.
- **Subscriptions are rare:** 57 of 971 products bill recurring.
- **Price anchors:** median $34.89, 75th percentile $74.25, 90th $222.02.

## Method, and what this cannot tell you

27 searches were run against Gumroad Discover and the top results of each captured with
a headless browser: asking price, currency, subscription flag, rating count, title.

**Rating count is a proxy for units sold, not a sales figure** — only some buyers rate, and that
share differs by category. Use this to rank categories against each other rather than to estimate
revenue. It is one snapshot rather than a trend, and it reflects the visible top of each category
rather than its full population. Nothing here is estimated or modelled beyond those limits.
