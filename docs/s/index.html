Who actually sells on Gumroad

4,545 sellers · 8,325 products · 205,250 ratings · 5 August 2026

Four things the seller column says that a product table cannot

  • Demand is more concentrated across sellers than most marketplace commentary assumes. The top 1% (45 sellers) hold 52.5% of all 205,250 ratings measured; the top 10% hold 89.2%; the remaining 4,091 sellers share 10.8% between them.
  • It is not a catalogue effect. The rank correlation between how many products a seller has and how much demand they attract is 0.284 — real, but weak. The median seller inside the top 1% has 2 products, and 14 of the 45 have exactly one. Listing more is not the mechanism.
  • One-product sellers are the marketplace. 3,263 of 4,545 sellers — 71.8% — have a single product here, and as a class they hold 31.2% of all ratings. The median seller has 1 product; the mean is 1.83, which is what a long tail does to an average.
  • 38% of sellers have never been rated at all — 1,710 of 4,545, across their entire measured catalogue. Not a single listing with a single rating. That is the outcome you are competing against, and it is the modal one.

Method, and what this cannot tell you

Sellers are derived from 8,325 distinct products keyed on product URL, collected by walking Gumroad's own 359-node category tree on 5 August 2026. Each node was taken up to three pages deep, capping it at 71 listings, and 166 of the 261 non-empty nodes hit that cap. So every product count here is a lower bound on that seller's catalogue, biased down for exactly the sellers whose listings rank deepest. Concentration figures are less affected than counts — a seller missing from the crawl is missing their ratings too — but they are still measured on what was found, not on the whole marketplace.

Ratings are a proxy for relative demand, not a sales figure. Only some buyers rate, that share differs by category, and no revenue estimate anywhere on this site is derived from them. 98 crawled nodes returned no listings and are excluded rather than reported as zeroes.

These are public commercial listings, published under public Gumroad storefront names and linked back to the storefront they came from. Nothing here is inferred about any person. If you run one of these storefronts and would rather not appear, open an issue on the repository and it comes out of the next build.
