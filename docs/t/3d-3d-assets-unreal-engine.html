Method

What this sample is. These are the 44 listings Gumroad ranked first in 3D > 3D Assets > Unreal Engine when the category was crawled on 5 August 2026, up to three pages deep. It is not a count of everything in the category, and nothing here supports a claim about category size — 187 of the 255 categories came back at the same 44-listing crawl cap.
