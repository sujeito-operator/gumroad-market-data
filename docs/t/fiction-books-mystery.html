Method

What this sample is. These are the 71 listings Gumroad ranked first in Fiction Books > Mystery when the category was crawled on 5 August 2026, up to three pages deep. It is not a count of everything in the category, and nothing here supports a claim about category size — 166 of the 261 categories came back at the same 71-listing crawl cap. What it does support is comparison: every category was sampled the same way, from the same ranking, on the same day. Prices are converted to USD at ECB reference rates for 2026-08-06.
