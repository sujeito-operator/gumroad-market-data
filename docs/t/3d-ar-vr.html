3D > AR/VR

Withdrawn — 2026-08-11 This page carried a median price, a 90th percentile, a seller count and a rating share for 3D > AR/VR. All of it was wrong, and the whole page has been removed rather than corrected.

Gumroad's discover endpoint does not return 404 for a category slug it does not recognise — it answers 200 and serves the site-wide default feed. This node is one of 6 in the published category tree that it does not recognise, so the listings recorded here were never in this category. They were other people's products, identical from one of these pages to the next.

The categories Gumroad does recognise are unaffected and are all here. What went wrong, and the test that now catches it, is in verify_taxonomy_nodes.py; the six are named in taxonomy-summary.json under unrecognised_node_correction.
