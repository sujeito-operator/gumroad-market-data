What the numbers say

  • A third of everything listed has never sold a measurable unit. 33% with zero ratings is the background rate you compete against.
  • Creative assets dominate volume. VRChat avatars are the only category sampled where every listing has ratings; Procreate brushes carry a median of 136 with a top product at 3,300.
  • Template and document categories look busy and move slowly. Excel dashboards 39% rated with a median of one rating; resume templates 42%; WordPress themes 43%; printable planners 44%.
  • Price and demand are close to unrelated. The highest-demand categories are among the cheapest.
  • Subscriptions are rare: 57 of 1511 products bill recurring.
  • Price anchors: median $34.89, 75th percentile $74.25, 90th $222.02.

Method, and what this cannot tell you

27 searches were run against Gumroad Discover and the top results of each captured: asking price, subscription flag, rating count. Rating count is a proxy for units sold, not a sales figure — only some buyers rate, and that share differs by category, so use this to rank categories against each other rather than to estimate revenue. It is one snapshot rather than a trend, and it reflects the visible top of each category rather than its full population.
